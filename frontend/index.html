Competition Rules

1. 2-Minute Blitz: Each participant is allotted exactly 2 minutes (120 seconds). The countdown begins as soon as the quiz is launched.

2. Speed & Accuracy: Answer as many questions as you can. Points are awarded instantly per question.

3. Tie-Breaker Hierarchy: In case of identical scores, rankings are determined by: (1) Higher Accuracy %, (2) Lower Time Used, (3) Earlier Submission.

4. Fair Play: Leaving the tab or refreshing will not pause the 120-second timer.
